About Our College

A learning environment dedicated to developing competent, ethical and skilled health professionals.

Our Commitment to Healthcare Education

Kingdome College Hub is established with a focus on health sciences education and professional development. Our aim is to support learners in gaining the knowledge, practical abilities and values required in healthcare.

We aspire to promote lifelong learning, responsible professional practice and quality healthcare services.

01

Our Vision

To be a recognised centre of excellence in health sciences education and professional development.

02

Our Mission

To provide quality learning experiences that develop competent, ethical and skilled healthcare professionals.

03

Our Core Values

Professionalism, integrity, compassion, accountability, teamwork and excellence.
