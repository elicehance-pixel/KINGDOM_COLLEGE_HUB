About KICOHAS

KICOHAS Healing Through Knowledge

Kingdom College of Health and Allied Sciences

Kingdom College of Health and Allied Sciences (KICOHAS) is committed to providing quality education in health and allied sciences through modern training, professional ethics and service for the community.

The college emphasizes excellence, integrity, compassion, professionalism, innovation, teamwork, respect and commitment in its educational mission.
